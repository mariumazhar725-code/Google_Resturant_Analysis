A Dataset slicer allows comparison between Train, Validation, and Test datasets.


💡 𝐊𝐞𝐲 𝐅𝐢𝐧𝐝𝐢𝐧𝐠𝐬:
1. Train contains substantially more reviews, businesses, and users than Validation and Test.
2. Average ratings remain highly consistent across all three datasets, ranging from 4.45 to 4.47.
3. Average reviews per business are higher in Train (3.12) than Validation (1.39) and Test (1.40).
4. The most-reviewed business has 210 reviews in Train, compared with 20 in both Validation and Test.
5. The 50–99 character range contains the largest number of reviews across all three datasets.
